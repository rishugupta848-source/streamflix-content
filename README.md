StreamFlix Content Analytics

Analyzed 15,000 subscribers and viewing data from 2017 to June 2026 to give StreamFlix’s CEO a management summary on retention, engagement and content investment. The analysis showed 75% of subscribers are still active, a 65.31% average completion rate, and Originals producing 27.2% of the roughly 3.3 million watch hours. I recommended a retention programme, moving budget toward Animation and Kids & Family (the best watch hours per $1,000), and timing big releases for quieter months.
